# 🧾 Multi-Tenant Retail SaaS Application

A **multi-tenant SaaS platform** built for retail businesses, designed to provide secure, scalable, and efficient POS and inventory management.  
Developed using **Laravel, Livewire, FilamentPHP, and Alpine.js**, this system enables business owners to manage sales, stock, expenses, payments, and financial reports — all in one place.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Multi-Tenant Architecture** – Separate and secure data for each client.  
- **Sales & POS Management** – Easy and fast sales processing.  
- **Inventory Management** – Real-time stock updates, returns, and damage tracking.  
- **Expense & Payment Tracking** – Manage all business finances effortlessly.  
- **Customer & Supplier Ledger** – Maintain transparent records for all transactions.  
- **User Management** – Roles & permissions for secure access control.  
- **Profit/Loss Reports** – Dynamic reports for performance insights.  
- **Bank Account & Due Management** – Full control over financials.  
- **Dynamic Units** – Custom units for different product types.  
- **SMS Notifications** – Instant alerts for important activities.  
- **Customizable Settings** – Configure business preferences without code changes.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Laravel 11, Livewire  
- **Admin Panel:** FilamentPHP  
- **Frontend:** Alpine.js  
- **Database:** MySQL  
- **Hosting:** Compatible with Shared/VPS/Cloud Servers
